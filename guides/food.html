Eating Well on a Student Budget

Your first year is busy: new classes, new friends, and long days on campus. It is easy to skip meals or spend your allowance on milk tea. Eating regular, simple meals helps you focus in class, and you do not need a big budget to do it.

Where to Eat Near Campus

Here are some usual go-to spots:

  • The school canteen for quick rice meals.
  • Carinderias outside the gate for affordable meals.
  • Your own baon from home.
  • Convenience stores for occasional snacks.

How to Plan Your Weekly Food Budget

  1. Check your allowance for the whole week.
  2. Set aside money for fare and school needs.
  3. Divide the remaining money by school days.
  4. Pack baon on at least two days.
  5. Keep a little money for emergencies.

Quick Tips

Always bring a refillable water bottle. Water stations on campus are free, and you will save a lot compared to buying bottled drinks every day.
